About Us

Our story

Bean There opened in 2022 with two machines and a big idea: coffee should be simple and friendly.

We brew at 93°C. An espresso used to cost 60 EGP, now only 45 EGP.*

* Price for a small cup.

How we brew a perfect cup

  1. Grind the beans fresh.
  2. Heat the water to 93°C.
  3. Pour slowly in circles.
  4. Wait 3 minutes and enjoy.
